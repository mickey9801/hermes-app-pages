Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a personal AI assistant that I (mickey9801) run on my own computer. It connects to my own Google Workspace account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts) through the official Google APIs, so that it can help me read and organise my own mail, files and schedule.

This application is not published, sold or distributed to the public. It is used solely by its developer, on a single account. No other person's Google data is ever accessed.

This site exists to satisfy the Google OAuth consent-screen branding requirements.
